## The idea


**The core idea is a mobile app that allows you to:**
- Connect with your hobbies and interest where you live, mainly through local communities 
- Share content, start discussions, nearby events or even host your event
- Communities are public (permissionless, permissioned) or private (permissioned)
    - Initially a set of groups are “spawned” for supported cities, feeding content from advertised events, local sports 
        - LOCAL Std themes: art, music, alternative, kids, trips, cars, motos,food, animals, financials, crypto, technology, etc 
        - Sports (practicing/following flag): football, tennis, basketball, volley, bike, skate, skii, paddle, horses
        - User created: non existent or too specific (private organizations, clubs, etc)
    - Currently many communities have one app for them, this would solve the platform / community issue 
- No AI algorithms, get content purely based on your choices and your explicit interests

**Open questions**
- All of the above looks good, but a core use case that sustains with a few users is needed. Local online communities could be the answer 
    - The goal is to replicate irc + forums communities during 1998-2005
    - What was the golden path, or main use case of IRC/old forums? 
    - Currently telegram/facebook groups somehow cover that gap, but have limitations
        - Groups are non-discoverable
        - Only one line of discussion
        - Not very dynamic/customizable 


## Cold start issue

How to resolve the cold star issue?

Some ideas:
- Provide index of local communities
- Allow users to propose extend that index, per administrator approval
- Allow users to create their own communities (ala IRC chat)
    -This would be the area that gets users on the platform, and eventually generates revenue. But the core value at the beginning looks weak for a revenue generation platform
