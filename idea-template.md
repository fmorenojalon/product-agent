## The idea

What are you thinking about building? Describe it in your own words — a sentence,
a paragraph, or a few bullet points. Don't worry about being precise yet.

## Why now

What triggered this idea? A customer conversation, a competitor move, a gap you
keep noticing, internal strategy shift? Any context about timing or motivation helps.

## What we already know

Any data points, customer quotes, internal research, or prior experiments that are
relevant. Paste them in directly — raw notes are fine.

## Who it's for

Who would use this? Describe the person, not a job title. What problem do they have
today? How are they solving it (or not solving it) right now?

## Constraints and considerations

Budget range, team size, timeline pressure, technical limitations, regulatory concerns,
partnerships that would be affected — anything that shapes what's realistic.

## Open questions

What are you most uncertain about? What would change your mind about pursuing this?

## Anything else

Competitors you're aware of, products that inspired this, links, screenshots, rough
sketches — whatever is in your head that might be relevant.
