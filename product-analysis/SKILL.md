Analyze the following points of the product in question:

* Core proposition that fulfills, and product portfolio
    * what's the main sector/vertical
        * Focus mainly on present information, you can add historic information but flag it as such. Products pivot their offerings, and that's ok but context is important
    * B2b, b2c or b2b2c
        * What are their main target profiles (within users or companies)
            * Are they developers? retail consumers, specific role within companies or departments?
            * This can be analyze for example with marketing messaging or available data of existing contracts/user base
    * Main use cases, what's the golden path or "killer app"?
    * Company information
        * Founding date
        * Country of foundation
        * Short brief of the founding team
        * Profits generated for the last 3 years. If profits are not available, show revenue
            *If neither profits nor revenue are available, show volume transacted in the last available year, use a core metric of their product that most close is to their revenue generation.
        * Raised money (if applicable)
* Main partners / integrations
    * Are these integrations already in production? how important are they within their core product offering. It's important to discern 'marketing' integrations from 'real-impact' integrations. We're not that interested in marketing integrations
    * Are integrations focused on expanding the product Reach (by tapping on new regions, or new communities/verticals) or expanding the existing product portfolio for the same Reach?
* Revenue model
    * What are their top revenue streams, how to the monetize
    * Is the product sustainable?
* Main regions
    * List or summarize where this product operates, and show which are their top 5 regions, and show the % for each region (% can be based on product usage or revenue generation)
* Legal stance
    * What are the Legal risks for this product? how does the company deal with them?
    * Do they currently have any potential regulation or compliance issue for the future? what regions are most affected?
* Main competitors
    * Who are their main competitors and why?
        * What is the differentiation?
* Technical stack
    * Summarize the components they need to run for the product, if this information is not public make your best guess. Also what languages they use for their sw stack.
    * if it's a blockchain related product
        * What chains they operate in?
        * do they operate crosschain?
        * How decentralized the product is?
            * This can be analyze in different planes: in terms of development work, governance, technical stack, hosting providers, etc. A short summary is enough.
* Most important releases in the last 2 years
    * What have been the latest investments and product releases?
* What are they planning for the next 12 months
    * What's coming up soon?
* What's the community sentiment for their products? Check online forums (like reddit) and user reviews sites (like apple store, android store, chrome store, etc)
    * We're specially interested about last 12 months feedback, older feedback is good to know, but should be categorized as such.


Once you have all information, present it like this:
* Provide a report summary in the chat window
* Generate the full report in pdf format
